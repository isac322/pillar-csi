────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
First-time configuration (cluster-scoped CRs)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Declare a storage target on each storage node:

   apiVersion: pillar-csi.bhyoo.com/v1alpha1
   kind: PillarAgent
   metadata:
     name: <storage-node-name>
   spec:
     nodeRef:
       name: <k8s-node-name>

2. Declare a pool (ZFS or LVM) on that target, a protocol (NVMe-oF/TCP),
   and a binding that produces the StorageClass:

   See https://github.com/bhyoo/pillar-csi#quickstart for the full YAML.

3. Watch readiness:

   kubectl get pillaragent,pillarstore,pillarprotocol,pillarstorageclass
   kubectl describe pillaragent <name>   # AgentConnected + Ready conditions
